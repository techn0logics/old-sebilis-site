Our Story

Old Sebilis is a Monsters & Memories guild built for players who miss the adventure, cooperation and community that made old-school MMORPGs special.

We're here to explore the world, build groups, overcome difficult encounters and create the kind of memories that come from actually knowing the people you're adventuring beside.

Whether you're an MMO veteran or completely new to this style of game, you're welcome here. We value good people, good attitudes and the journey more than rushing to the destination.

The Old Sebilis Way

Community First

We want a guild where players know one another, group together and help each other experience the world.

No-Box

Our focus is real players adventuring together rather than controlling armies of boxed characters.

New Player Friendly

Questions are welcome. Learn the game, explore at your pace and grow alongside the rest of the guild.

Monsters & Memories Resources

A few community-made tools and references that can make life in Aêthoril a little easier.

Monsters & Memories Wiki

A broad community reference for game information. This is a good first stop when you want general information about the world, classes, systems, quests, locations, and other player-documented knowledge.

Best for: general research and learning the game.

Visit the Wiki

MNM Realm

A community database with searchable information for items, NPCs, quests, and other game data. It also tracks recent additions and provides a community contribution system for submitting or correcting information.

Best for: looking up NPCs, quests, items, and recently updated data.

Visit MNM Realm

Bindloc Item Compendium

A detailed item and drop database with filters for class, race, equipment slot, zones, and other useful criteria. Bindloc also includes class and spell reference tools.

Best for: comparing gear, researching drops, and planning upgrades.

Open Bindloc

MnM Drops

A fan-made item database focused heavily on loot. Search items and filter by slot, class, race, stats, weapon type, zone, and more. It also provides zone and mob pages so you can trace where particular items come from.

Best for: finding who drops an item and deciding where to farm.

Visit MnM Drops

MnMdb

A fan-made Monsters & Memories database centered around community drop and vendor information. It also includes maps, a quest board, and advanced search tools.

Best for: vendor research, drop information, maps, and quest hunting.

Visit MnMdb

Official Monsters & Memories Site

The official source for development updates, class deep dives, announcements, world information, media, and other information directly from the Monsters & Memories team.

Best for: official news, development updates, and confirmed game information.

Visit Official Site

These are community resources and may occasionally contain incomplete or outdated information. When in doubt, check official Monsters & Memories announcements or ask another guild member in Discord.
